Three steps from daydream to departure

No affiliate clutter, no 40-tab research spirals. Voyage guides are built to be used in this order.

Step 01

Pick the season

Every guide opens with a month-by-month chart — crowd levels, weather and price — so you go when it's actually good, not just when you're free.

Step 02

Map the route

Day-by-day itineraries with real transit times and walkable distances. Copy ours, or remix it into your own loop with the notes provided.

Step 03

Pack light, go slow

Region-specific packing lists and a one-bag philosophy. Fewer things to carry means more time looking up at where you actually are.

About the author

I'm Maren — and I travel embarrassingly slowly.

Nine years ago I quit a design job in Copenhagen, sold most of what I owned, and started writing down what I wished someone had told me before each trip. Voyage grew out of those notebooks.

I don't chase 30-countries-in-30-days. I'd rather learn one valley properly — the early bus, the family trattoria, the trailhead the guidebooks miss. Every guide here is walked before it's written.
